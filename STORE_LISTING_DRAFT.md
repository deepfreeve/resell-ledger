## Apple App Store

- **Name:** CACHE
- **Subtitle:** Inventory, sales & profit
- **Suggested category:** Business
- **Promotional text:** Keep reseller inventory, costs, sales, and profit organized in one workspace.
- **Description:**

  CACHE helps independent resellers keep track of what they buy, what they list, and what each sale earns.

  - Organize inventory across product categories, conditions, SKUs, and storage locations.
  - Record purchase cost, listing price, sale price, marketplace fees, and shipping.
  - Track pending payouts, completed sales, profit, and reinvestment allocations.
  - Import eBay sales reports and review previous sales before adding them.
  - Save private item photos, receipts, and notes with your inventory.
  - Export sales and create backups of your records.
  - Review sales and profit by date, category, and platform.

  CACHE is a record-keeping tool. Review imported details, estimates, and generated suggestions before relying on them. CACHE does not provide tax, accounting, or legal advice.

- **Keywords draft:** reseller, inventory, sales tracker, profit, eBay, resale business
- **Screenshots needed:** dashboard, inventory with photos, add-item flow, sale and reinvestment split, sales reports, eBay import review, account/privacy controls. Use real release-build screens with sample data only.
