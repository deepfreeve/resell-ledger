## What CACHE does

CACHE is a reseller inventory and sales record-keeping tool. It helps organize inventory, costs, sales, shipping, fees, and estimates. It does not provide tax, accounting, legal, investment, or financial advice, and its calculations and OCR/AI suggestions can be incomplete or incorrect. Review records and calculations before relying on them.

## Your account and records

Keep your sign-in credentials secure and enter information you have the right to store. You are responsible for the accuracy of your inventory, cost, sales, and listing information; complying with marketplace rules; and keeping independent backups of important records. CACHE provides export and backup tools, but they do not replace your own record-retention practices.

## Optional integrations

Some features depend on third-party providers and may change or be unavailable. Their terms, availability, and data practices apply when you use them. AI-generated listing text, active listing results, and market-price data require your review and are not guaranteed to be accurate, complete, licensed for every use, or current.

Deletion removes your CACHE account and associated records, subject to provider backup retention and any legal retention obligations disclosed in the final Privacy Policy.
