SALES HISTORY

Import eBay sales CSV

Choose an eBay orders/sales CSV or Listings Sales report and review rows before importing. Enter the bought cost and choose what percent of profit is reinvested. Sale costs (purchase cost, eBay fees, shipping, and other costs) are returned to reinvestment first; only profit left after all costs is split. Leave cost blank if unknown. We match by SKU or title, skip duplicates, and ignore buyer details. Listings Sales reports only give a date range, so those sales use the report end date.
